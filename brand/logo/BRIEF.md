# SkinScan AI logo brief

**Name:** SkinScan AI (one word "SkinScan", capital S twice, then "AI").

**What it is:** a skin-lesion triage tool for clinicians, nurses and medical students. It reads a dermoscopic
photo with the patient's details, suggests how soon to refer, and keeps each lesion's history. It runs on
the device, works offline and does not diagnose.

**Audience:** clinicians in clinics with poor internet, medical students, and the Congressional App
Challenge judges who will see it in a two-minute video.

**Adjectives:** careful, precise, private, calm, clinical without being cold.

**Category and competitors:** SkinVision, Miiskin, DermEngine, MoleScope, FotoFinder, Skin Analytics (DERM),
Google DermAssist.

**Category clichés to avoid:** magnifying glasses, scan brackets around a dot (the old logo), shields, medical
crosses, stethoscopes, a literal mole or person silhouette, neural-network nodes, AI sparkles, teal-to-blue
gradients.

**Constraints:**
- Brand colour is gentian violet `#5A2CB0`, the ink of the surgical skin marker (see `docs/DESIGN.md`).
- Must read at 16 px (favicon), as an app icon, in one colour, reversed on violet, and in the film.
- No gradients, no shadows.
- Wordmark set in Mona Sans (SIL OFL, so logo use is allowed), width 115, weight 800, converted to outlines.

**Decision-maker:** Jason Xiao.

## Word map

| Start | Leads to |
| --- | --- |
| skin | surface, spot, mole, pigment, a ring of skin under the dermatoscope lens |
| triage | sort, first, priority, the odd one out |
| scan | lens, ring of light, dermatoscope, look closely |
| marker | the violet dots a surgeon draws around a lesion before a biopsy |
| follow over time | contour lines, growth, visits |
| ugly duckling | eight moles alike, one different |

The strongest intersections are the **marker ring** (violet dots circling a spot), the **odd one out** (the
ugly duckling sign) and **contours** (a spot's edge at each visit).

## Longlist

1. **Circled spot:** a spot ringed by marker dots, the mark a surgeon draws before a biopsy.
2. **Lens ring:** a dermatoscope lens seen face-on, its ring of LEDs around one spot.
3. **Odd one out:** a 3 by 3 grid of round dots where one dot is larger and not round, the ugly duckling sign.
4. **Contours:** a spot with two outlines drawn around it, wider on one side, the lesion at three visits.
5. **Triage stack:** three bars of different lengths, a priority queue.
6. **Pin on skin:** a body-map pin whose head is a lesion.
7. **S from two spots:** an S made from two offset discs, one hollow.
8. **Pigment net:** a small honeycomb, the dermoscopic pigment network, in a circle.
9. **Wordmark tittle:** the dot of the i in "SkinScan" replaced by a circled spot.
10. **Half and half:** a disc split into photo and data halves.

Scored for idea clarity, distinction from the category, simplicity and strength at 16 px, the three
strongest and most different are **1 (Circled spot)**, **3 (Odd one out)** and **4 (Contours)**.
2 is a weaker version of 1, 5 and 6 are generic, 7 and 10 need explaining, and 8 breaks up at small sizes.
9 is kept as a detail of the wordmark for whichever symbol is picked.
